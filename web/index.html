SVM FLEET System User Guide

Welcome to SVM FLEET Operating System

SVM FLEET is an executive fleet management suite built for commercial taxi aggregators (Uber, Rapido, Ola) across Hyderabad. It manages 30 vehicles, 27 active drivers, and 3 major regional depots.

Role Switching

Use the top branch dropdown to view Hyderabad (All Hubs) as Fleet Owner, or isolate specific stations like Moosapet Central Hub, Kukatpally, or Madhapur.

Live Telemetry

Click the bell icon in the top header to inspect real-time shift submissions, automated sync alerts from Uber/Rapido, and physical cash deposits.

How to Log a Shift with Searchable Driver Select

  1. Click the purple "+ Add Shift" button in the top navigation or Duty Register.
  2. In the Select Driver combobox, type the driver's name, phone, or cab registration (e.g. "Ravi" or "3368"). The driver and vehicle are populated automatically!
  3. Choose 12 Hrs Shift (Min 10 trips) or 24 Hrs Shift (Min 18 trips).
  4. Enter trips completed, gross earnings, fuel spent, and cash collected from riders.
  5. The Official Commission Engine dynamically computes the tier, credit, and whether the company pays the driver or the driver owes cash to the office.

Duty Register & Printable Driver Vouchers

The Duty Register lists every completed shift log with financial breakdowns.

  • Search & Filter: Use the search box or hit Ctrl + K to find any shift by driver, plate, or mobile number.
  • Receipt Button: Click the receipt icon on any row to generate a printable, formatted Driver Settlement Voucher for paper filing or driver WhatsApp confirmation.
  • Export CSV: Click "Export CSV" to download a spreadsheet of all shift records for accounting.

Managing Vehicles, Drivers & Regional Hubs

  • Add Driver: Go to the Drivers tab and click "+ Add Driver" to register new operators with license numbers, emergency contacts, and assigned cabs.
  • Add Vehicle: Go to the Vehicles tab and click "+ Add Vehicle" to enter commercial taxi registration plates and models.
  • Transfer Hub: Click "Transfer Hub" on any vehicle card to instantly reassign a cab and driver between Moosapet, Kukatpally, or Madhapur.
  • Add Regional Depot: Click "+ Add Hub & Manager" in the sidebar or hub picker to expand operations into new Hyderabad zones (e.g. Gachibowli, Secunderabad).
